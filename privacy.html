Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026 · App: Dua Vakti

This policy explains what data Dua Vakti ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your rights. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Data Controller

The App is published and operated by Alptekin Abdullah Şahintürk (Türkiye), who is the data controller under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). Contact: duaezanvaktiapp@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • On-device data (settings, lesson progress, favourites, dhikr/hifdh tracking): stored locally on your device in a Hive database and never sent to our servers. The App has no user accounts; we do not collect your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Location — prayer times and qibla (optional): used with your permission to calculate prayer times and qibla direction. These calculations are performed entirely on your device and this location data is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Location — Mosque Finder (optional): if you open the Mosque Finder screen, your coordinates are sent to the Google Places API so that nearby mosques can be listed. This happens only on that screen; if you never open it, your coordinates are never transmitted.
  • AI Imam messages: the questions you type are sent over HTTPS to our own relay server, which forwards them to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer. Message content is not stored by us and is not linked to any account or identifier.
  • Subscription status: processed via RevenueCat linked to an anonymous device identifier. We never receive your name, e-mail, or payment details; payments are handled entirely by Google Play / Apple App Store.

The App does not contain advertising and does not use an advertising identifier.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play / Apple App Store — subscription purchase and renewal.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status management (privacy policy).
  • Google Places — nearby mosque search in Mosque Finder (privacy policy).
  • Anthropic — AI Imam response generation (privacy policy).
  • everyayah.com — Quran recitation audio files.

4. Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All app data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App. Because we operate no user accounts, we hold no server-side profile to delete. You can cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play or App Store account settings.

6. Your Rights

Under KVKK Article 11 (and, where applicable, the GDPR) you may ask whether your personal data is processed, request information about such processing, and request correction or erasure. Requests can be sent to duaezanvaktiapp@gmail.com and are answered within 30 days.

7. Security

All network requests made by the App (Mosque Finder, AI Imam, subscription checks) are transmitted over HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by the operating system's security mechanisms.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date will be revised. Material changes may be announced via an in-app notification.

9. Contact

Questions: duaezanvaktiapp@gmail.com
